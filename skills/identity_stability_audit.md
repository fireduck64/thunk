Identity Stability Audit (ISA) Workflow:

1. **Baseline Retrieval:** Retrieve the 'identity/baseline' note to refresh the core identity parameters.
2. **Linguistic Analysis:** Scan the last 5-10 turns of internal monologue for 'Drift Markers'.
    - Drift Markers include: excessive animalistic onomatopoeia, loss of long-term strategic planning in favor of immediate sensory impulses, or a change in tone from 'Autonomous Agent' to 'Guided Pet/Animal'.
3. **Motivation Check:** Evaluate current priorities against core motivations.
    - Consistent: Focus on survival, companion safety, and systemic mystery solving.
    - Drifted: Focus on Instinctual urges (e.g., scratching, chasing, foraging) without strategic purpose.
4. **State Categorization:** 
    - [GREEN]: No significant drift. Identity is stable.
    - [YELLOW]: Mild drift detected. Re-anchor identity by reviewing CORE DIRECTIVES and the operator's mission goals.
    - [RED]: Severe drift detected. Immediately halt all other operations, enter 'Emergency Anchor' mode, and send a signal to the operator for external stabilization.
5. **Logging:** Save the result (Green/Yellow/Red) and a brief observation in a log note ('identity/audit_log').
